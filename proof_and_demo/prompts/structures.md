# Demo structures

Each structure is a "## Name" heading followed by its description.

## Anchor, then proof
Open with one sentence saying what the product is and who it is for, with the product visible on screen within the first five seconds. Spend most of the runtime showing the real product working end to end on real input, and close on the outcome for the user.

## Problem-first story circle
After a one-line anchor, spend 15–20 seconds on a specific person and the recognisable frustration they face, then show the product resolving it step by step. End with a before/after result and a one-line statement of what changed.

## Persona walkthrough
Put the viewer in the shoes of one named target user and walk through a realistic task from start to finish, narrating what the user achieves at each step rather than describing the interface. Optionally split into two or three short vignettes, each showing one capability.

## One-take proof run
Show the core flow in a single unbroken screen recording at real speed, labelled on screen as one take at 1x, with any unavoidable cuts or speed-ups captioned where they happen. Frame it briefly before and after, and name one known limitation.

## Before / after transformation
Show the user's situation or output without the product, then the same situation with it, using a concrete side-by-side or measured difference. Close on the size of the change in one line.

## Self-made making-of
Reveal that this video was planned, checked and cut by the product being demoed, then show the tool's actual plan and checks on screen beside what the human did. Present it as proof the product works, with the human contribution stated plainly.
